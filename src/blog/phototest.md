I desperately needed a holiday during the latest quarter of 2024. The news about my potential unemployment in 2025, going into an LDR after a few years of marriage, and the unfortunate developments in Indonesia got me frantically searching for a destination. Emotionally feeling numb, I knew I wanted to go somewhere unfamiliar enough to get me into beginner-level survival mode. I just needed to feel something to know that I’m still alive. Yeah, 2024 was that awful.

D-7, I book a one-way ticket to Vietnam. Reason? News about big tech companies investing in Uncle Ho’s land and not mine. This is a country whose performance will surpass Indonesia's anytime soon, thanks to its investment in education and tech. So in short, part curiosity and part disappointment in my government. Also, I’ve worked with Vietnamese during my time at Glints… all the more reason to see how they live.

I was quite nervous. I didn’t have a well-thought-out itinerary, nor did I speak the language. All I have is prior surface-level knowledge of the country. It won against the war with the US, is a communist country, is on its track to become a developed country, and has pho. And so I shape my trip around that.
